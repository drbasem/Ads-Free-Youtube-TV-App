Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

What the app accesses

If you choose to sign in with Google, ElHalawany Tube requests these permissions:

  • Basic profile (name, email, profile picture) to show which family member is signed in.
  • YouTube read-only access (youtube.readonly) to list the channels you subscribe to and the videos you liked.

The app cannot upload, delete, comment, like, subscribe, or change anything on your account.

How the data is used and stored

  • All data stays on your own device (TV or phone). Nothing is sent to any server owned by the developer.
  • The app talks only to Google and YouTube services to fetch your subscriptions, liked videos, and video streams.
  • Favorites, watch history, and playback positions are saved locally on the device.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ElHalawany Tube's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Remove your profile inside the app (Library → sign out / delete), which deletes the stored tokens and revokes access. You can also revoke access at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.
