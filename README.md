# ElHalawany Tube

Website for **ElHalawany Tube**, a personal, non-commercial Android TV and Android phone app for watching YouTube with the family.

This repository hosts the public pages Google requires for the app's OAuth consent screen:

| Page | Link |
|---|---|
| Home page | [index.html](index.html) |
| Privacy policy | [privacy.html](privacy.html) |

## About the app

- **Google sign-in on the TV**: scan a QR code or enter a short code at `google.com/device` from your phone. No typing passwords on the TV remote.
- **Multiple users**: each family member signs in with their own Google account and switches profiles from a "Who's watching?" screen.
- **Subscriptions feed**: shows the latest videos from the channels you subscribe to, plus your liked videos.
- **Recommendations**: suggested videos based on what you watched, liked, and saved.
- **Favorites and resume**: save videos to favorites and continue any video from where you stopped.
- **HD playback**: choose video quality up to 1080p and higher, remembered between videos.

## Privacy in short

- The app requests **read-only** YouTube access (`youtube.readonly`) plus basic profile info.
- It cannot upload, comment, like, subscribe, or change anything on your account.
- All data (sign-in tokens, favorites, watch history) stays **on the device**. Nothing is sent to any developer server, sold, or shared.
- You can revoke access at any time from [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Full details are in the [privacy policy](privacy.html).
