ElHalawany Tube

ElHalawany Tube is a personal, non-commercial Android TV and Android phone app for watching YouTube videos for a single family.

Users can optionally sign in with their Google account so the app can show videos from the YouTube channels they are subscribed to and the videos they liked. Access is read-only: the app never uploads, comments, likes, or changes anything on the user's YouTube account.

Several family members can each sign in on the same TV and switch between their profiles. Favorites and "continue watching" progress are stored only on the device.
